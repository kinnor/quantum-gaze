Multi-Modal Document Intelligence & OCR Extraction Engine

AI Platform Framework

Manual document processing costs enterprises thousands of hours and introduces costly human errors. Our Multi-Modal Document Intelligence Engine leverages vision models and fine-tuned LLMs to extract, validate, and structure complex invoices, legal contracts, bills of lading, and regulatory filings into clean REST APIs in seconds.

Agentic AI Framework
