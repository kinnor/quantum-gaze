AI Workflow Products for Freight, Customs, EDI & Enterprise Integration

Three human-reviewed workflow products built from 24+ years of freight, customs and integration architecture — delivered as a decision-grade pilot or production-limited workflow in 14–21 days, then production-hardened through explicit follow-on gates.

ClearanceGuard AI — Customs Document Preflight

Flagship Workflow Product

ClearanceGuard AI cross-checks commercial invoices, bills of lading, packing lists, entry summaries and shipment data before filing, and routes every discrepancy to a human reviewer with an evidence trail. Built for Canadian and Canada–US customs brokers, freight forwarders and importers with 50 to 500 employees. The system recommends; an authorized employee decides.

What it does:

  • Cross-Document Checks: Invoice, bill of lading, packing list, entry summary and shipment data are reconciled against each other before anything is filed.
  • Human Reviewer Queue: Discrepancies land in a reviewer queue with the source pages and the reason for the flag, so a clearance officer can decide in minutes.
  • Explicit Boundaries: No automatic filing, no tariff-classification or legal advice, no unsupervised release. Deployable in a client-controlled Canadian cloud tenant or on-premise.

EDI ExceptionDesk

EDI Operations Workflow

EDI ExceptionDesk correlates X12 acknowledgements (997/999), transaction errors (204/214/210/990), partner implementation guides and integration logs so operations teams can resolve EDI failures without waiting for a specialist. Built for CIOs, integration directors and EDI managers at mid-market 3PLs, forwarders, carriers and manufacturers.

What it does:

  • Acknowledgement Correlation: 997/999 acknowledgements are matched to the original transactions and to partner guides, so a rejection reads as a plain-language cause rather than a segment code.
  • Transaction Error Triage: 204, 214, 210 and 990 failures are grouped by partner, root cause and business impact, with the suggested fix and the evidence behind it.
  • Resolution With Oversight: Every recommendation is logged with its reasoning; an operator confirms the fix and the audit trail stays with the case.

BizTalk Estate Miner

Modernization Workflow

BizTalk Estate Miner turns BizTalk exports — bindings, schemas, maps, orchestrations and logs — into a migration inventory, dependency graph, risk register and wave plan. Built for CIOs and enterprise architects who run BizTalk and need a defensible modernization plan before committing budget.

What it does:

  • Migration Inventory: Every artifact, port, partner and schema is catalogued with its traffic profile and ownership.
  • Dependency Graph & Risk Register: Hidden couplings, custom pipelines and unsupported components surface as ranked risks, not as surprises during cutover.
  • Wave Plan: A sequenced plan that lets the legacy backbone and the target platform (Azure, Kafka, .NET) run side by side until each wave is proven.
