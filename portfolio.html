Customs Document Preflight — the Foundation of ClearanceGuard AI

Commercial Invoices, Bills of Lading, Packing Lists & Entry Summaries

AI Workflow Product

Challenge: Discrepancies between commercial invoices, bills of lading, packing lists and entry data were surfacing only after filing, creating rework, holds and reviewer overload at brokers and forwarders.

Solution: Designed a document preflight workflow that cross-checks the shipment file before filing and routes every discrepancy to a human reviewer with an evidence trail — the system recommends; an authorized employee decides.

Impact: Productized as ClearanceGuard AI: no automatic filing, no tariff classification or legal advice, no unsupervised release — a bounded, human-reviewed pilot covers 4 document classes, 1 business unit and up to 300 validation pages.

Customs Brokerage Leader — API & Electronic Trade Infrastructure

X12 / EDIFACT Gateways for CBSA & CBP Transmissions

Cross-Border Trade

Challenge: Manual document handling and failed or delayed electronic transmissions caused border clearance holds and specialist bottlenecks.

Solution: Developed robust integration pipelines connecting customs brokerage systems with CBSA/CBP electronic data standards (X12 and EDIFACT).

Impact: Reliable electronic trade transmissions with structured exception handling — the operational pattern that later shaped EDI ExceptionDesk, which correlates 997/999 acknowledgements and 204/214/210/990 errors so ops teams resolve failures without waiting for a specialist.
