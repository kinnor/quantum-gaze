AI Workflow Pilots & Engagement Model

What can Quantum Gaze realistically deliver in 14–21 days?

A decision-grade pilot or production-limited workflow in 14–21 days: a working, human-reviewed workflow on a bounded scope (for example, four customs document classes in one business unit), with a baseline, acceptance tests and an evidence trail your leadership can decide on. Production hardening, live integration and recurring operation follow through explicit gates. We do not promise to build anything in two weeks, and we do not call a pilot production-ready.

How is an engagement structured and priced?

Every engagement follows the same proof-led ladder, so you commit one step at a time:

  • Free 20-minute triage call: go/no-go and a data checklist.
  • Solution Blueprint ($5,000 CAD): a paid 48-hour workshop delivering architecture, baseline, acceptance tests and a fixed pilot statement of work; credited to the pilot if signed within 30 days.
  • Bounded pilot ($27,500–$35,000 CAD): a working, human-reviewed workflow on a defined scope.
  • Hardening ($15,000–$50,000 CAD): production controls and live integration.
  • Recurring ($2,500–$5,000 CAD per month): hosting, monitoring, rule and model governance, support.

What does ClearanceGuard AI do, and what does it not do?

ClearanceGuard AI is a customs document preflight. It cross-checks commercial invoices, bills of lading, packing lists, entry summaries and shipment data before filing, and routes discrepancies to a human reviewer with an evidence trail. It does not file entries, does not give tariff-classification or legal advice, does not certify compliance and never releases a shipment on its own. The system recommends; an authorized employee decides.

How do EDI ExceptionDesk and BizTalk Estate Miner fit in?

EDI ExceptionDesk correlates X12 acknowledgements (997/999), transaction errors (204/214/210/990), partner guides and logs so your operations team can resolve EDI failures without waiting for a specialist. BizTalk Estate Miner turns BizTalk exports (bindings, schemas, maps, orchestrations, logs) into a migration inventory, dependency graph, risk register and wave plan. Both follow the same Blueprint, pilot, hardening and recurring ladder, and both keep a person in charge of every decision.
