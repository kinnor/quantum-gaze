Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed insights on BizTalk migration timelines, EDI architecture, and public procurement

BizTalk Modernization & Architecture

What is the timeline for Microsoft BizTalk Server 2020 sunset?

Microsoft BizTalk Server 2020 mainstream support ends in April 2028 (with extended support running through April 2030). Furthermore, the SBMP Service Bus adapter is retired on September 30, 2026. Enterprise planning, architecture validation, and wave testing typically require 12–18 months, meaning organizations must finalize their migration roadmap within the active 19.5-month budget cycle.

How does Quantum Gaze de-risk legacy BizTalk migrations?

We utilize a Paid Discovery Assessment ($15k–$35k) to catalog all orchestrations, custom C# pipeline components, and partner schemas. We then execute a wave-based migration to Azure Integration Services (AIS) and Logic Apps with a parallel Coexistence Architecture, ensuring zero transaction loss and immediate fallback capability.

EDI & Domain Capabilities

Which transaction sets and EDI standards do you support?

We are certified in ANSI X12 standards with two decades of production experience in Transportation (204, 214, 210, 990), Customs Brokerage (309, 350), Supply Chain (850, 855, 856, 810), and international EDIFACT (CUSDEC, CUSRES, ORDERS, INVOIC) over AS2, SFTP, and RESTful APIs.

How does your client-side EDI sandbox protect data privacy?

Our interactive parser operates 100% client-side in your local browser JavaScript engine. Zero bytes of your sample EDI or payload data are transmitted to our servers or third-party APIs, ensuring complete enterprise data privacy and NDAs compliance.
