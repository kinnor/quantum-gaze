Rapid AI Prototyping & Engineering

How does Quantum Gaze deliver a working AI prototype in only 2–3 weeks?

By combining 24+ years of foundational software architecture mastery with modern AI coding agent pipelines and modular microservice blueprints. Rather than spending months on theoretical slide decks, we work directly with your stakeholders to define core schemas, orchestrate agent graphs, and deploy a working, testable full-stack prototype (Python/FastAPI, Next.js, Vector DB) in 14–21 days.

What is Agentic Process Automation, and how does it differ from traditional bots?

Traditional RPA bots follow brittle, rigid if/then rules that break whenever document formats or edge cases change. Agentic AI utilizes coordinated networks of intelligent LLM-driven agents (via LangGraph and Model Context Protocol) that dynamically reason, inspect errors, interact with databases/APIs, and adapt to unpredictable edge cases with built-in verification and human-in-the-loop oversight.

AI Coaching & Developer Enablement

How does AI Developer Coaching work for existing engineering teams?

We provide hands-on bootcamps and workflow integration for your software engineers. We train your team on using AI coding agents safely, prompt-driven architecture, automated test synthesis, and code review governance — multiplying developer productivity by 3x–5x while maintaining strict code quality and security standards.

How do you protect intellectual property and enterprise data privacy?

We enforce strict zero-data-leakage architectures: client-side in-browser evaluation, dedicated private cloud tenants (Azure Canada Central / AWS Montreal), and on-premise open-weight model deployments (Llama 3.3, Mistral, DeepSeek). Your proprietary data is never exposed to public models or third-party training pipelines.
