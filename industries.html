🌐 Freight, Customs, EDI & Enterprise Integration

Industries We Build AI Workflow Products For

Senior-led AI workflow products for customs brokers, freight forwarders, 3PLs, manufacturers and Canadian public-sector organizations — a decision-grade pilot or production-limited workflow in 14–21 days, with a human reviewer in the loop.

Customs Brokers & Importers

ClearanceGuard AI, our flagship customs document preflight, cross-checks commercial invoices, bills of lading, packing lists, entry summaries and shipment data before filing, and routes every discrepancy to a human reviewer with an evidence trail. No automatic filing, no tariff-classification or legal advice: the system recommends, an authorized employee decides. Built for Canadian and Canada–US brokers, forwarders and importers with 50–500 employees.

Freight Forwarding, 3PL & Carriers

EDI ExceptionDesk correlates X12 acknowledgements (997/999), transaction errors (204/214/210/990), partner guides and logs so your operations team resolves EDI failures without waiting for a specialist. Backed by 24+ years of integration work, including a Top-5 Canadian freight forwarder platform and CargoWise, Descartes, Sterling, MuleSoft and Kafka environments.

Automotive & Manufacturing

Supplier and dealer EDI, middleware and ERP integration for manufacturers, including BMW ecosystem integrations. For organizations still running BizTalk, BizTalk Estate Miner turns your exports (bindings, schemas, maps, orchestrations, logs) into a migration inventory, dependency graph, risk register and wave plan before you commit to a platform.

Government & Public Sector (Federal / QC)

Human-reviewed AI workflows and integration modernization for Canadian federal and Québec public bodies, delivered bilingually under Bill 96. Registered in the Fichier des fournisseurs du Québec (no 711938 OM), AMP authorized and qualified for CanadaBuys ProServices Streams 1 & 3, which permits directed contracts under $40,000.
